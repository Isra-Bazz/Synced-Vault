## Parts of Costume
1. Boots 
2. Morph Suit
3. Shoulder Pads
4. Belt
5. Helmet
6. Gloves 
## Method
### Boots
These will be made using pleather (fake leather) and wrapping around a black boot (kmart). After making a pattern by wrapping my leg with plastic wrap and tracing.
Making 1 seam through the back (maybe zippering) and then gluing the pattern on the boot surface.
Making the gold accent on the boot lip by folding the pleather over itself, painting it gold after which it will be sewn on to the outside of the lip of the boot.
### Morph Suit
Paint a black morph suit with Gold Dimensional Fabric Paint. 
Do so by tracing pattern of the costume with clothing chalk as it will wash out without impacting paint
### Shoulder Pads
3D Model the Pads (hard but doable)
### Belt
Craft belt by using EVA foam sheets glued together in a thick stack. After which make a sleeve from gold poplin or whatever fabric gives a gold sheen akin to the boots.
3D printing the belt portion as a clasp (looks easy asf)
### Helmet
2 Options, time based. 1 - Buy from [here](https://www.printables.com/model/1614062-black-dino-power-ranger-helmet-casco-negro-de-powe) 2 - Make own and print (likely look shittier and harder to do)
### Gloves
Cheap Amazon gloves that will tuck in under the morph suit and under the 3D printed wrist clasps. They will have the gold back hand accents as 3D prints and will be glued on.
